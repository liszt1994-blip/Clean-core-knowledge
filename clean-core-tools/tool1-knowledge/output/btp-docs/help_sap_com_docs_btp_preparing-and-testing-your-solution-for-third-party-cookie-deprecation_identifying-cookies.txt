Identifying Cookies

While going through your critical user journey, watch the Network and Application tabs of the developer tools for cookie usage. If cookies are third-party, they are likely affected by the tracking protection.

The affected cookies could be set by your application or SAP BTP software. SAP will take action to solve the cookie-related issues for cookies set by SAP BTP. For all other cookies, it is your responsibility to adapt your application.

Both SAP-provided software developed on BTP as well as extension scenarios on BTP use SAP technology and thus work with similar cookies. To distinguish between them – and get a high-level understanding of who is responsible for reacting to the third-party cookie pahse-out – consider the hostname that attempts to set or read the cookies.

Below is a table with typical components and examples of their common cookies included with BTP-based software you might encounter during testing, that might be affected by the third-party cookie phase-out. Other cookies that you encounter most likely originate from your application and must be handled by you.

Important are the name of the cookie and the domain. Cookies with the same name can occur for different domains.
